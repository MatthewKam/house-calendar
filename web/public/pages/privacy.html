Sprig

Privacy

Last updated November 16, 2026

The short version: Sprig keeps your family's calendar, tasks, photos and kids' progress so your family can use them. We never show ads, never sell or rent your information, and never use it to track you. You can delete your account, and your household, from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Sprig is a family app at sprig-family.com. Questions about this page or your information go to hello@sprig-family.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and email address, and which way you sign in (Apple, or a code we email you). If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • What your family puts in: people's names, colors and birthdates, events, tasks, lists, checklists, stars and jars, rewards, meals and recipes, reading logs, learning game progress, photos and videos, and your home address if you add it.
  • Your devices: which devices are signed in (for example "iPhone, Safari") and when they were last used, so you can see them and sign them out. If you turn on alerts, the address your phone's notification service gives us for sending them.
  • Guardian PINs are stored scrambled (hashed), never as the digits themselves.
  • Calendar connections: if you connect Google Calendar or iCloud, the access we need to sync them, stored encrypted.

We don't keep a history of where you are. When you allow it, your device's rough location (about a kilometer) is used to show the weather where you are, and isn't saved.

Children

Sprig is made for families with young children, and grown-ups are in charge of it. A guardian creates the household and adds children; young children use a device a guardian has added with a code and don't have accounts or email addresses. Older children can be invited by a guardian to sign in themselves.

What we keep about children is what their guardians put in: their name, birthdate, tasks, stars, reading, learning progress and family photos. It's used only to run the app for their family. Children never see ads, can't buy anything, and can't leave kid mode without a guardian's PIN. A guardian can see, change or delete anything about their children at any time, or delete the whole household.

A guardian adding a child agrees, as their parent or guardian, to what this page describes. Questions or requests about a child's information go to hello@sprig-family.com.

Who else handles it

We use a few services to run Sprig. Each gets only what it needs for its job:

  • Fly.io runs our servers in the United States and stores your family's information.
  • Tigris keeps encrypted backups of it, so it isn't lost if something breaks.
  • Resend sends the sign-in and PIN reset codes to your email address.
  • Apple signs you in, if you choose it. Apple and Google sync your calendars, if you connect them.
  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude) reads what you hand it: a flyer you photograph or something you say for Snap a flyer and Say it, a recipe page you add, dish ideas you ask for, and event titles when sorting synced events by person. Anthropic doesn't use this to train its models.
  • Google Maps works out drive times and suggests addresses, from the addresses you type.
  • Open-Meteo and the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder turn a place into a weather forecast; they get the place, not who you are.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla's notification services deliver alerts to your phone, if you turn them on.

We don't use analytics or advertising services, and nothing in Sprig follows you to other apps or websites. We'd only share information beyond this if the law required it.

Who in your family sees what

Your household's information is shared with the people in it. Guardians can see and change everything; kids' devices and the family wall show what's meant for them, and need a guardian's PIN to change things.

Deleting

  • Your account: Settings → Users → Delete my account. Your sign-ins, devices and calendar connections are deleted at once.
  • Your household: if you're its account holder, deleting your account deletes the household too, with everything in it (calendar, tasks, photos, everything). To keep it for your family, make another guardian the account holder or a co-holder first. If you're not the holder, you leave the household, and what your family added stays theirs.
  • Backups: deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 30 days, then it's gone.
  • Anything in particular (a photo, an event, a person) can be deleted in the app at any time.

Can't get into the app? Email hello@sprig-family.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it for you.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Calendar access is stored encrypted, PINs and codes hashed, and sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to what Sprig needs.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the last change.
