Sprig

Privacy

Last updated November 27, 2026

The short version: Sprig keeps your family's calendar, tasks, photos and kids' progress so your family can use them. We never show ads, never sell or rent your information, and never use it to track you. You can delete your account, and your household, from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Sprig is a family app at sprig-family.com, made and run by Tayno. Questions about this page or your information, and requests about a child's information, go to hello@sprig-family.com.

What we keep

  • Your account: your name and email address, and which way you sign in (Apple, Google, a code we email you, or a passkey). A passkey stays on your device; we keep only its public half, which can't sign in by itself. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we only see Apple's relay address.
  • What your family puts in: people's names, colors and birthdates, events, tasks, lists, checklists, stars and jars, rewards, meals and recipes, reading logs, learning game progress, photos and videos, and your home address if you add it.
  • Your devices: which devices are signed in (for example "iPhone, Safari") and when they were last used, so you can see them and sign them out. If you turn on alerts, the address your phone's notification service gives us for sending them.
  • Guardian PINs are stored scrambled (hashed), never as the digits themselves.
  • Calendar connections: if you connect Google Calendar or iCloud, the access we need to sync them, stored encrypted, and a copy of their calendars and events so your family sees them in Sprig (see Google Calendar below).

We don't keep a history of where you are. When you allow it, your device's rough location (about a kilometer) is used to show the weather where you are, and isn't saved.

Google Calendar

If you connect a Google account to Sprig, with your permission Sprig:

  • Sees your email address, to show which Google account is connected.
  • Reads your list of calendars, so you can choose which ones to show in Sprig, and whether each one can be changed.
  • Reads the events in the calendars you choose (title, time, place and notes) and keeps a copy, to show them to your family on the calendar, Today and the wall.
  • Adds, changes or deletes events only when someone in your household does it in Sprig — for example moving an event, or adding one to a Google calendar. Sprig never changes your Google Calendar on its own.

Google Calendar information is used only to show and update your family's calendar in Sprig, and is shown only to the people in your household. We don't sell it, use it for ads, or use it to train AI models, and people at Sprig don't read it except to fix a problem you ask us to look at, for security, or where the law requires it. It's shared with another service only to run a feature you use: an event's place may be sent to Google Maps to work out the drive time, as described below. It's never sent to an AI service.

AI. Sprig doesn't use Google user data, or anything made from it, to develop, improve or train AI or machine learning models, and never sends it to one. Who each event is for is worked out on our server from the family names in its title, without AI.

Keeping it safe. Your Google access is stored encrypted, the copy of your events is kept on our servers' encrypted storage and in encrypted backups, and everything travels over HTTPS. Only the people in your household can see it in Sprig.

How long we keep it. While Google Calendar is connected, Sprig keeps its copy in step with Google each time it syncs, so an event deleted in Google is removed from Sprig too. To disconnect, remove the account in Sprig under Settings → Calendars; its access and the copy of its events are deleted at once, and the same happens when you delete your account or household. Deleted information can stay in our backups for up to 30 days, then it's gone. You can also remove Sprig's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Sprig's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

Sprig is made for families with young children, and grown-ups are in charge of it. A guardian creates the household and adds children. Young children use a device a guardian has added with a code, and don't have accounts or email addresses. Older children can be invited by a guardian to sign in themselves.

What we keep about a child, and where it comes from:

  • From their guardians: their name, color, birthdate, stage and allergies, their tasks, goals, rewards and the moments a guardian catches them being good, and the events they're on.
  • From the child, as they use Sprig: tasks they tick, stars they put in jars, potty chart punches, reading and learning game progress, list items they add or tick, and photos they upload from their device.
  • From a child's device: its name (for example "iPad, Safari") and when it was last used, and, if alerts are on, the address its notification service gives us for sending them.
  • An older child who signs in themselves: their name and email address, and how they sign in, as for any account.

It's used only to run Sprig for their family: to show it to their family and keep it in step on their devices. We don't show children ads, don't sell or share their information, and children can't buy anything or leave kid mode without a guardian's PIN. The only others who handle it are the services under Who else handles it, each because Sprig needs it to work, and none of them may use it for anything else. A child's information is never sent to Anthropic (the AI service) from a child's device; only a guardian can use Sprig's AI features.

A guardian's consent. Before the first child is added to a household, Sprig emails the guardian a short notice of what it keeps about children and a code. Entering the code gives their consent, as the child's parent or guardian, and Sprig emails to confirm it and how to take it back. We don't ask a child for more than Sprig needs, and nothing a child does in Sprig depends on giving more.

A guardian can, at any time:

  • See everything about their child in the app: their tasks, stars, jars, moments and history on the Tasks and Rewards pages, and their details in Settings → Family.
  • Change or delete any of it in the app, or remove the child: Settings → Family → the child → Remove, which deletes what we have about them (see How long we keep it).
  • Take back their consent: Settings → Family → Children's privacy → Take back. The children's devices are signed out, and no more children can be added, until a guardian gives it again. Removing the children deletes what we have about them.
  • Ask us to do any of this, or anything else about their child's information, at hello@sprig-family.com.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as Sprig needs it for your family:

  • While someone is in your household, what your family puts in is kept, so you can look back (a child's stars over the months, what they've read), until you delete it.
  • Activity history (ticked tasks, moments, potty chart punches and learning sessions) older than 2 years is deleted automatically.
  • A device unused for 6 months is signed out, and its alert address deleted.
  • A person removed from the household: their tasks, stars, jars, rewards, moments, goals, progress and devices are deleted at once. Photos and events they're in stay with the family, without their name on them, until the family deletes them.
  • A household or account deleted: everything in it, at once (see Deleting).
  • Backups: deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 30 days.
  • Our server's logs, which our host keeps for a few days to find problems, don't include your information or where you connect from.

Who else handles it

We use a few services to run Sprig. Each gets only what it needs for its job:

  • Fly.io runs our servers in the United States and stores your family's information.
  • Tigris keeps encrypted backups of it, so it isn't lost if something breaks.
  • Resend sends the sign-in and PIN reset codes to your email address.
  • Apple or Google signs you in, if you choose it. Apple and Google sync your calendars, if you connect them.
  • Anthropic (the maker of Claude) reads what you hand it: a flyer you photograph or something you say for Snap a flyer and Say it, a recipe page you add, and dish ideas you ask for, each only when you ask. Nothing from your calendars goes to it. Anthropic doesn't use this to train its models.
  • Google Maps works out drive times and suggests addresses, from the addresses you type and the places on your events.
  • Open-Meteo and the U.S. Census Bureau's geocoder turn a place into a weather forecast; they get the place, not who you are.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla's notification services deliver alerts to your phone, if you turn them on.

We don't use analytics or advertising services, and nothing in Sprig follows you to other apps or websites. We'd only share information beyond this if the law required it.

Who in your family sees what

Your household's information is shared with the people in it. Guardians can see and change everything; kids' devices and the family wall show what's meant for them, and need a guardian's PIN to change things.

Deleting

  • Your account: Settings → Users → Delete my account. Your sign-ins, devices and calendar connections are deleted at once.
  • Your household: if you're its account holder, deleting your account deletes the household too, with everything in it (calendar, tasks, photos, everything). To keep it for your family, make another guardian the account holder or a co-holder first. If you're not the holder, you leave the household, and what your family added stays theirs.
  • Backups: deleted information can remain in our backups for up to 30 days, then it's gone.
  • Anything in particular (a photo, an event, a person) can be deleted in the app at any time.

Can't get into the app? Email hello@sprig-family.com from the address you sign in with, and we'll delete it for you.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and is stored encrypted on our host's disks and in backups. Each family's information is kept apart from every other's. Calendar access is stored encrypted, PINs and codes hashed, and sign-in codes expire after 10 minutes. We keep a written security program, with someone in charge of it, and look at it again each year. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep what we hold to what Sprig needs, and if something went wrong we'd tell the families affected.

Changes

If we change this page in a way that matters, we'll say so in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows the last change.
