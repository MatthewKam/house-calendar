Common questions

How does someone else join our family?

A guardian opens Settings → Users and invites them. A grown-up (or an older kid with their own phone) signs in and enters the code; a young child's tablet just enters the code, with no account.

How do I set up the family wall?

Settings → Users → Add a wall display gives you a code to type on the wall's screen. The wall asks for a guardian's PIN to open, and again to change anything.

I forgot my PIN.

On your own phone, open Settings → Users, tap Change it, then Forgot PIN? and Email me a code. The account holder can also reset another guardian's PIN.

How does my child get out of kid mode?

Kid mode only opens again for a guardian's PIN, so little ones can't wander into the grown-up side.

Alerts aren't reaching my phone.

Turn them on in Settings → Notifications on your own phone. On iPhone, Sprig needs to be added to the Home Screen first (Share → Add to Home Screen) and opened from there.

How do I delete my account?

Settings → Users → Delete my account. If you hold the household's account, the household is deleted too, unless you make another guardian the account holder first. See Privacy for what's kept and what's deleted.
